AstroWander
Hold it up to the sky

The camera view gets the catalogue drawn over it, so you can point at something and read off what it is. Phone compasses are typically 5–15° out, so drag sideways once to line the labels up with what you actually see — that nudge is remembered.

—

Identify a photograph

Give it a picture of the night sky and it works out where the camera was pointing — by finding star patterns in the image and matching their geometry against the catalogue. It needs roughly eight or more real stars in frame. A few seconds of exposure on a steady surface is usually enough; a snapshot of a bright city sky will not be. Everything runs on this device, and no image is uploaded anywhere.
